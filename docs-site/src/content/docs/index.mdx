Nuits Receipts is a receipt inbox that runs on Cloudflare Workers. You deploy your own copy into your own Cloudflare account, and Nuits never sees your data.

**Home** holds every finance document you add: receipts, sales invoices, bank statements, other finance documents and the months you sent. They sit in folders that build themselves (by year and month, seller, client, kind and tag), searchable by their details and by the words inside PDFs, and they open in the app's own PDF viewer. See [Find any document](/how-to/find-any-document/).

Above the documents, Home shows the month you are closing, in three steps: **Check**, **Match** and **Send**.

- **Check.** It reads the seller, amounts, VAT and dates from the receipts you add or forward by email, and asks you only about what it is unsure of.
- **Match.** It imports your bank's CSV export (you map the columns once), matches the sure pairs for you, and shows likely ones side by side for you to confirm. It lists the payments that have no receipt yet.
- **Send.** It prepares a ZIP of the month's originals with an index spreadsheet, or just a CSV, for you to send to your accountant.

Every resolving action, such as approving a receipt or marking a payment as not a purchase, comes with **Undo**. You can also install the app on your phone or computer; see [Install the app](/how-to/install-the-app/).

![Home for September 2026, with the search field, the setup checklist at 3 of 5 done, the Check, Match and Send steps with Match showing 1 missing, and the archive folders beside the month's files](../../assets/screenshots/home.png)

:::note[What it is not]
Nuits Receipts organises receipts and prepares files for your accountant. It is not tax, VAT or accounting advice, and it is not a bookkeeping system. Figures are read by AI and you check them. Your accountant decides how things are booked. Keep originals as your local law requires. Nuits Receipts keeps a copy in your own Cloudflare R2 bucket.
:::
