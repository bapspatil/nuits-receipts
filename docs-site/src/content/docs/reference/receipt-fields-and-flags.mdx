Receipts (`kind` `expense`) and sales invoices (`kind` `sales`) are stored in the same table and use the same fields, statuses and flags. Fields marked "sales" exist only on sales invoices.

"Read" fields come from Clairic, cross-checked against labelled lines in the document text. Any value you type in is kept when the document is read again.

## Approving

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Button | **Approve receipt** (**Approve invoice** for a sales invoice), in the bar at the bottom of the page |
| Reason field | **For your accountant**, under **Note**, prefilled with "Checked against the original". Required. |
| Required facts | Seller, issue date, a supported currency, total, amount before VAT and VAT amount. Sales invoices also need the client. |
| Result | Status `ready`, review reasons cleared, `reviewed_at` set. The flags present at approval are remembered and not raised again for the same values. |

## Statuses

| Status | Shown as | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pending` | Reading | Stored, not yet read. |
| `processing` | Reading | Being read. A read holds a 120-second lease; after it expires another read may take over. |
| `ready` | Ready, or Matched when matched to a bank row | No open review flags, or approved. |
| `needs_review` | Needs you | At least one review flag is open. |

A document with status `needs_review` whose only flag is `missing_eur` can still be matched to a bank row. A match to a EUR bank row clears that flag.

## Review flags

The app shows one card per problem. Codes that describe the same problem share a card. The raw codes are in the `review_reasons` export column.

A receipt is in **Receipts To do** while it has a required flag: one that asks for the seller, date, currency, total, before-VAT or VAT amount (and the client on a sales invoice). Other flags are optional: they stay on the receipt as a note and never block **Approve receipt**.

### Missing or unsure values

| Code | Card title | Group | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `missing_vendor` | Who is this from? | Seller | Seller is empty. |
| `uncertain_vendor` | Who is this from? | Seller | Seller was read but not settled (see below). |
| `missing_issue_date` | No date on it. | Date | Issue date is empty. |
| `uncertain_issue_date` | No date on it. | Date | Issue date read but not settled. |
| `missing_currency` | Which currency? | Amounts | Currency is empty. |
| `uncertain_currency` | Which currency? | Amounts | Currency read but not settled. |
| `unsupported_currency` | We don't know this currency. | Amounts | Currency is not one of EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, SEK, NOK, DKK, CAD, AUD, JPY. |
| `missing_total` | What was the total? | Amounts | Total is empty. |
| `uncertain_total` | What was the total? | Amounts | Total read but not settled. |
| `missing_net` | Amount before VAT is missing. | Amounts | Amount before VAT is empty. |
| `uncertain_net` | Amount before VAT is missing. | Amounts | Amount before VAT read but not settled. |
| `missing_vat` | VAT amount is missing. | Amounts | VAT amount is empty. |
| `uncertain_vat_amount` | VAT amount is missing. | Amounts | VAT amount read but not settled. |
| `missing_vat_rate` | What VAT % was charged? | Amounts | Total, net and VAT are known, VAT is not 0, and the rate is empty. |
| `uncertain_vat_rate` | What VAT % was charged? | Amounts | VAT rate read but not settled. |
| `missing_invoice_number` | No receipt number. | Details | Receipt or invoice number is empty. |

A read value is **settled** when the document text states it on a labelled line, when Clairic and the text agree, when Clairic is confident (confidence at least `REVIEW_MIN_CONFIDENCE` and `p_null` at most `REVIEW_MAX_UNCERTAINTY`) and the text does not contradict it, or when net plus VAT equals the total and ties it to settled values. `uncertain_*` is raised for seller, issue date, currency and total when they are missing or unsettled, and for net, VAT amount and VAT rate only when they were read but are unsettled. Saving an approval with a reason drops `uncertain_*` flags on later reads.

### Arithmetic

| Code | Card title | Group | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `vat_mismatch` | The numbers don't add up. | Amounts | Net plus VAT differs from the total by more than 2 minor units. |
| `vat_rate_mismatch` | VAT doesn't match the rate. | Amounts | Net is not 0, a rate is known, and VAT differs from net times the rate by more than 2 minor units and by more than 0.1 % of net. |
| `credit_or_sign` | This looks like a refund or credit note. | Amounts | Total, net or VAT is negative. |

`missing_vat_rate`, `vat_mismatch`, `vat_rate_mismatch` and `credit_or_sign` are checked only when total, net and VAT are all known.

### Euro amount

| Code | Card title | Group | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `missing_eur` | No euro amount yet. | Euro amount | A non-EUR document with a total and issue date has no ECB reference rate, no euro amount typed in, and no match to a EUR bank row. |

| Code | Card title | Group | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `uncertain_product` | Which part of the business? | Filing | Clef's product probability is below `REVIEW_MIN_CATEGORY_CONFIDENCE` and its top choice differs from the default product. The default is used. |
| `uncertain_expense_type` | What kind of cost is this? | Filing | Same, for the expense type. `other` is used. |
| `classification_failed` | We couldn't file this automatically. | Filing | The Clef call failed or returned an invalid answer. |

## Email intake results

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Sender not approved, and DMARC passed for the From domain or SPF passed for the envelope sender | Raw email stored and shown in **Receipts To do** as a **New sender** row. Not read. Logged as `sender_not_allowed` with the domain only. |
| Sender not approved and not proven | Dropped. Logged as `sender_auth_failed` or `sender_unverified`. Nothing stored. |
| Sender ignored from Receipts To do | Logged as `sender_ignored`. Nothing stored. |
| Same Message-ID already waiting or read | Logged as `duplicate`. Nothing stored. |
| 50 Unknown sender emails waiting, or 10 from this sender | Logged as `held_limit`. Nothing stored. |
| From address approved and DMARC passed for its domain | Read like mail from an approved envelope sender. |
| Email over 20 MiB | Dropped. Logged as `email_too_large`. |
| SPF `fail` or `softfail`, or DMARC `fail`, in Cloudflare's `Authentication-Results`, or a `Received-SPF` envelope sender that differs | Dropped. Logged as `sender_auth_failed`. |
| No proof of sender | Raw email stored with `sender_unverified`. Not read. |
| Supported attachments (PDF, PNG, JPEG, WebP) | Each up to 10 stored and read as its own document. PNG images of 2 pixels or less in either direction are ignored. |
| No supported attachment, to the receipt address | The email body (HTML, or plain text) is stored and read as the document. |
| No supported attachment, to the invoices address | Raw email stored with `invoice_no_attachment`. |
