Use this guide at the end of each month. The **Month** page walks you through three steps: **Check receipts**, **Match payments**, and **Send to your accountant**. The last one builds a ZIP with every original receipt, named by date and seller, plus an index spreadsheet. The app prepares the files; your accountant decides how they are booked.

The examples use the default accountant name, "your accountant". If you changed **Accountant** in **Settings** > **Export**, the buttons use your wording.

## Prerequisites

- Receipts for the month added and reviewed. See [Review and approve a receipt](/how-to/review-and-approve-a-receipt/).
- The month's bank statement imported and matched. See [Match payments and receipts](/how-to/match-payments-and-receipts/). You can send without this, but the package then says it is not ready.

## Send the month

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the month">

Select **Month** in the top bar, or **Close October** on **Home**. Use the month switcher at the top right to pick another month.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the first two steps">

**Check receipts** lists receipts that still need you, with **Review N receipts**. Receipts with no date belong to no month until you add one; follow **Add the date**.

**Match payments** lists payments with no receipt, with **Find receipts**. When both steps show **Done**, the third step is stamped **Ready**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the ZIP">

In **Send to your accountant**, check the file list and the **Spent** total, then select **Download October ZIP**. The button shows the size for smaller months.

If you only need the spreadsheet, select **Spreadsheet only (CSV)** instead.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the folder">

Unzip it (Mac: double-click; Windows: **Extract all**). You get a folder such as `receipts-2026-10`. Send that folder to your accountant. For a shared OneDrive or SharePoint folder, upload the folder itself (**Upload** > **Folder**), not the ZIP: OneDrive's web page does not unzip.

These steps are also under **Then, send it to your accountant** on the page.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it as sent">

Select **I've sent October to your accountant**. The app records which receipts you sent, so it can tell you if anything changes later.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send a month that is not ready

If receipts still need a check, no bank statement is imported, or payments have no receipt, the step is stamped **Not ready** and says why. You can still send what is there:

- **Download anyway** puts unchecked receipts, and emails held for review, in a `check-these` folder, marked as needing review in the index. The button says how many, for example **Download anyway (includes 2 unchecked)**.
- Tick **Also include N receipts with no date** to add undated receipts to `check-these` as well.
- Payments with no receipt are listed in a separate spreadsheet in the package, so the gap is visible.

Tell your accountant which rows still need checking. Review flagged rows first; your accountant decides how these are booked.

## Send changes after sending

If a receipt dated in the month is added, corrected or removed after you marked it as sent, the step is stamped **Changed** and says what changed, for example "Changed since you sent it on 3 Nov: 1 added and 1 corrected."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download only what changed">

Select **Download only what changed (N files)**. The ZIP is named like `receipts-2026-10-changes.zip` and holds only the new and corrected receipts. Or select **Download everything again** for a full package.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and mark it again">

Send the files, then select **I've sent October to your accountant** again.

  </Step>
</Steps>

A changes ZIP has its own index spreadsheet that lists only the changed files. Tell your accountant so they do not replace the full index with it. A corrected receipt can get a new file name, so the old file stays in their folder unless they remove it.

## Check that it worked

- A banner says "Marked October as sent." and the step is stamped **Sent** with the date.
- On **Home**, the month card shows the **Sent** stamp and the last progress step reads "Sent" with the date.
- The step's summary shows **Last sent** with the date.

## Limits and options

- One package holds up to 900 files. A bigger month shows **This month is too big for one package.**; download the spreadsheet and fetch the originals from the app.
- A line of your own under the download button comes from **Note under the month download** in **Settings** > **Export**. See [Change settings in the app](/how-to/change-settings-in-the-app/).
- To upload the files straight to a OneDrive folder instead of downloading, see [Upload the month to OneDrive](/how-to/upload-the-month-to-onedrive/). An upload marks the month as sent when it finishes.
