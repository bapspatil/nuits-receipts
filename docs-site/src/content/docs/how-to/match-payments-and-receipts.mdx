Use this guide to make sure every payment out in a month has a receipt, or a reason not to. The app matches payments automatically across every imported month and marks obvious non-purchases; this guide covers the rest.

## Prerequisites

- A bank statement imported for the month. See [Import a bank CSV](/how-to/import-a-bank-csv/).
- The receipts for that month in the app. Receipts still in **Review** are matched too and show **Needs you** next to the match until you approve them. See [Review and approve a receipt](/how-to/review-and-approve-a-receipt/).

## Match the missing payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the missing payments">

Select **Bank** in the top bar (on a phone, the tab bar at the bottom) and pick the month. It opens on **Missing** when any payment out has no receipt; **All** shows every payment. The **Match** row on **Home** links here too, from **Find receipts**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the likely receipt">

When a receipt looks like the payment, it is shown beside it as **Likely**, with the seller, date and amount. A small table puts the **Bank** and **Receipt** values side by side under **Compared**, so you can see what differs.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Match it, or pick another">

If it is the right receipt, select **Match**. If it does not fit the usual rules (for example a card payment in another currency, or a payment booked long after the receipt date), the app says why and the button is **Match anyway**.

If it is the wrong receipt, open **Pick another receipt** and type the seller, date or amount. With no likely receipt, the payment says "No receipt fits." and shows the same search box and **Add receipt**. Choose one and select **Match**. You can also paste a receipt ID there.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the rest at once">

When several payments have an exact likely receipt, **Will match N payments** above the list shows each pair and why. Select **Match all N likely** (or **Match the likely one**) to match them together.

The payment now shows a **Receipt** link instead of the **Missing** stamp.

  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also match from the receipt side. A receipt page shows **Payment?** when an unmatched payment fits it; select **Match** (or **Match anyway**).

If no receipt exists yet, the payment says "No receipt found yet." with **Add receipt**. Upload it; it is matched as soon as it is read.

## Mark payments that need no receipt

Some money out has no receipt: tax payments, loan repayments, money you pay yourself. Many are marked for you on import, with a label such as **Tax**, **Bank fee** or **Cash** next to **Not a purchase**; the import banner's **Undo not a purchase** turns them all back. For the rest, select **Not a purchase** on the payment. It now shows **Not a purchase** and no longer counts as missing. The banner offers **Undo**.

To reverse it, open the payment's **More** and select **Needs a receipt**.

For money moved between two of your own accounts, pair the two sides instead, so both are explained together. See [Pair an own-account transfer](/how-to/pair-an-own-account-transfer/).

## Undo a match

On a matched payment, open **More** and select **Remove match**. The payment counts as missing again.

## Check that it worked

- The line at the top of **Bank** reads, for example, "12 out, 12 matched, 0 missing", and the page says **Every payment has a receipt.** with **Send** and the month, for example **Send September**.
- On **Home**, the **Match** row shows **Done**.
- If other months still have gaps, **Bank** links them under "Also missing", for example "Also missing: August (2)".

:::caution[Editing a matched receipt]
Saving any change to a receipt removes its link to a bank payment, then automatic matching runs again. A match you made by hand, or one with an exception, has to be made again. Check **Bank** after correcting a matched receipt.
:::

A seller that appears on your bank statement under a different name (for example through a payment processor) may never match automatically. A vendor rule with **Bank name** fixes that; see [Sort costs with vendor rules](/how-to/sort-costs-with-vendor-rules/).
