Use this guide to make sure every payment out in a month has a receipt, or a reason not to. The app matches payments automatically when exactly one approved receipt fits; this guide covers the rest.

## Prerequisites

- A bank statement imported for the month. See [Import a bank CSV](/how-to/import-a-bank-csv/).
- The receipts for that month approved. Only approved receipts can be matched. See [Review and approve a receipt](/how-to/review-and-approve-a-receipt/).

![The Bank page with matched payments and one payment missing a receipt](../../../assets/screenshots/payments.png)

## Match the missing payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the missing payments">

Select **Bank** in the top bar (on a phone, the tab bar at the bottom) and pick the month. Select **Missing** to show only payments out with no receipt. The **Match payments** step on **Home** links here too, from **Find receipts**, and says how many likely matches are waiting.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the receipt">

Each missing payment has **Find the receipt** (the first one is open). In **Which receipt is it?**, likely matches are listed first under **Likely**, and every other approved, unmatched receipt under **Other ready receipts**. Choose one.

To use a receipt ID instead, open **Paste a receipt ID instead** and fill in **Receipt ID**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm exceptions if needed">

If the amount or date does not fit the usual rules (for example a card payment in another currency, or a payment booked long after the receipt date), tick **The amount or date doesn't line up exactly, and that's fine**. Without it, the app refuses a match that does not fit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Match">

Select **Match**. The payment's stamp changes to **Matched**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also match from the receipt side. A receipt page shows **Is this the payment?** when an unmatched payment fits it; select **Yes, match**.

If no approved receipt exists yet, the payment shows **Add the receipt**. Upload it, approve it, then come back.

## Mark payments that need no receipt

Some money out has no receipt: tax payments, loan repayments, money you pay yourself. Open **Find the receipt** on the payment and select **Not a purchase (no receipt needed)**. The stamp changes to **Not a purchase** and the payment no longer counts as missing.

To reverse it, select **Needs a receipt after all**.

For money moved between two of your own accounts, pair the two sides instead, so both are explained together. See [Pair an own-account transfer](/how-to/pair-an-own-account-transfer/).

## Undo a match

On a matched payment, select **Remove match**. The payment counts as missing again.

## Check that it worked

- The summary at the top of **Bank** shows, for example, "12 payments out", "12 with a receipt" and "0 missing", and the page says **Every payment has a receipt.**
- On **Home**, the **Match payments** step shows **Done**.
- If other months still have gaps, **Bank** lists them under "Also missing receipts in".

:::caution[Editing a matched receipt]
Saving any change to a receipt removes its link to a bank payment, then automatic matching runs again. A match you made by hand, or one with an exception, has to be made again. Check **Bank** after correcting a matched receipt.
:::

A seller that appears on your bank statement under a different name (for example through a payment processor) may never match automatically. A vendor rule with **Name on your bank statement** fixes that; see [Sort costs with vendor rules](/how-to/sort-costs-with-vendor-rules/).
