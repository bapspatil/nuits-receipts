Use this guide to make sure every payment out in a month has a receipt, or a reason not to. The app matches payments automatically when exactly one approved receipt fits; this guide covers the rest.

## Prerequisites

- A bank statement imported for the month. See [Import a bank CSV](/how-to/import-a-bank-csv/).
- The receipts for that month approved. Only approved receipts can be matched. See [Review and approve a receipt](/how-to/review-and-approve-a-receipt/).

![The Bank page with matched payments and one payment missing a receipt](../../../assets/screenshots/payments.png)

## Match the missing payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the missing payments">

Select **Bank** in the top bar (on a phone, the tab bar at the bottom) and pick the month. It opens on **Missing** when any payment out has no receipt; **All** shows every payment. The **Match** row on **Home** links here too, from **Find receipts**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the receipt">

Each missing payment has a receipt picker. Likely matches are listed first under **Likely**, and every other approved, unmatched receipt under **Other ready receipts**. Choose one.

To use a receipt ID instead, open the payment's **More** and paste it in **Receipt ID**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm exceptions if needed">

If the amount or date does not fit the usual rules (for example a card payment in another currency, or a payment booked long after the receipt date), tick **Not exact, still this**. Without it, the app refuses a match that does not fit.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Match">

Select **Match**. The payment now shows a **Receipt** link instead of the **Missing** stamp.

  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also match from the receipt side. A receipt page shows **Payment?** when an unmatched payment fits it; select **Match**.

If no approved receipt exists yet, the payment says "No receipt found yet." with **Add receipt**. Upload it, approve it, then come back.

## Mark payments that need no receipt

Some money out has no receipt: tax payments, loan repayments, money you pay yourself. Select **Not a purchase** on the payment. It now shows **No receipt** and no longer counts as missing.

To reverse it, open the payment's **More** and select **Needs a receipt**.

For money moved between two of your own accounts, pair the two sides instead, so both are explained together. See [Pair an own-account transfer](/how-to/pair-an-own-account-transfer/).

## Undo a match

On a matched payment, open **More** and select **Remove match**. The payment counts as missing again.

## Check that it worked

- The line at the top of **Bank** reads, for example, "12 out, 12 matched, 0 missing", and the page says **Every payment has a receipt.**
- On **Home**, the **Match** row shows **Done**.
- If other months still have gaps, **Bank** links them under "Also missing", for example "Also missing: August (2)".

:::caution[Editing a matched receipt]
Saving any change to a receipt removes its link to a bank payment, then automatic matching runs again. A match you made by hand, or one with an exception, has to be made again. Check **Bank** after correcting a matched receipt.
:::

A seller that appears on your bank statement under a different name (for example through a payment processor) may never match automatically. A vendor rule with **Bank name** fixes that; see [Sort costs with vendor rules](/how-to/sort-costs-with-vendor-rules/).
