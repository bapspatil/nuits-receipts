Use this guide to clear the receipts waiting in **Review**. A receipt lands there when the reader is unsure about a field, when the numbers do not add up, or when something about the source needs a look. Only approved receipts can be matched to a payment and go in the month's main folder.

## Prerequisites

- At least one receipt in the app. See [Add receipts](/how-to/add-receipts/).
- The original to hand, or a screen big enough to see the receipt page's preview beside the form.

![A receipt page marked Needs you, with the original on the left, the flagged questions on the right and Approve receipt in the bar at the bottom](../../../assets/screenshots/receipt-review.png)

## Review a receipt

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the queue">

Select **Review** in the top bar (on a phone, the tab bar at the bottom). The title says how many are waiting, for example **4 to check**. The list shows undated receipts first, then the oldest, each with its amount and a one-word reason; hover or long-press the reason to see it in full. With more than five, narrow it with **Missing info**, **Check the amount** or **Email not verified**. Select the first row to start. A row that is not a business receipt can be set aside from the list with **Discard**; see below.

Mail from senders who are not on your list is shown first, under **Unknown senders**. Select **Allow** to read that email and any others waiting from them (their later mail is read as it arrives), **Ignore** to delete their mail and stop keeping it, or **More** > **Delete email** to remove just that email.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the flags with the original">

The original is on the left, under **The original**, and the flags come first on the right, under a heading such as **2 to check**. Each flag is a short question, such as **What was the total?** or **The numbers don't add up.**, with the field to fix beside it. Images show inline beside the form; for a PDF, select **Open PDF** and keep it beside the page.

Everything else the reader found is under **Other fields**. Correct any field there too, including **Part** and **Kind**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in what approval needs">

Approval needs the seller, date, currency, total, before-VAT and VAT amounts. Enter `0` in **VAT** if the receipt has no VAT. Leave an amount empty only if you truly do not know it; the receipt then cannot be approved.

If your bank charged a different euro amount for a foreign-currency receipt, fill in **In euros** and **Why** under **Other fields**. Both are needed together.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the note and approve">

Under **Note**, **For your accountant** starts as "Checked against the original". Change it if you want to say more, such as why you accepted a refund. It cannot be empty when you approve. Then select **Approve receipt** in the bar at the bottom of the page.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the next one">

The bar shows where you are, for example **1 of 4 to check**. **Approve receipt** opens the next receipt in Review, and **Skip ›** leaves this one and opens the next. After the last one, **Review** says **All checked** and offers the month's next step, such as **Import statement**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set a receipt aside

If something is not a business receipt, select **Discard** on the receipt page or on its row in **Review**. Pick a reason (**Personal**, **Duplicate**, **Not a receipt** or **Other**) or select **Discard** again to skip it. The receipt leaves Review, totals, matching and the month's package, but its file is kept. If it was matched to a payment, the app asks **Remove match?** first.

The banner offers **Undo**. Later, find it on **Receipts** under **Discarded** and select **Restore**.

To clear several at once, select **Select** on **Review**, tick the rows and pick a reason.

## Have it read again

Select **More** > **Read again**. The reader runs on the saved original again; anything you typed in is kept and wins over the new reading. Flags such as **Reading failed.** and **The reader stopped early.** show the same button inside the flag.

If the PDF has no readable text (usually a scan), select **More** > **Upload a copy** and upload a photo of it. That adds a new receipt; mark the old one as its duplicate afterwards. See [Resolve duplicate warnings](/how-to/resolve-duplicate-warnings/).

## An email the app could not verify

An email flagged **We couldn't verify who sent this email.** was kept but not read. Download it with **More** > **Download original** and check that it is genuine. If it is, select **Read it**. If it is not, leave it unapproved: it stays out of the month's main folder. If reading it does not work, see [Troubleshoot](/how-to/troubleshoot/).

## Check that it worked

- The banner says **Approved.** on the next receipt, and the **Review** count in the top bar (or the bottom tab bar on a phone) goes down. When nothing is left, **Review** says **All checked**.
- The approved receipt loses its **Needs you** stamp, or shows **Matched** once a bank payment is linked. Its button is now **Save**.
- On **Home**, the **Check** row shows **Done** when nothing dated in that month needs you.

Saving a ready receipt checks it again. If the change raises a new problem, it goes back to Review with "Saved. It needs another look, so it moved back to Review." Saving changes to a receipt also removes its link to a bank payment, then automatic matching runs again. Check **Bank** afterwards. See [Match payments and receipts](/how-to/match-payments-and-receipts/).
