Use this guide when you move money between two of your own accounts, for example from your main account to savings. Both sides show up as payments: one out, one in. Pairing them tells the app neither needs a receipt, and keeps the reason on record.

## Prerequisites

- Both accounts set up in the app, and statements imported for both, so that each side of the transfer is in the app. See [Import a bank CSV](/how-to/import-a-bank-csv/).
- The two sides are in different accounts, with the same amount and currency (one out, one in).
- Neither side is matched to a receipt or invoice, and neither is marked **Not a purchase**. If one is, select **Remove match** or **Needs a receipt after all** on **Payments** first.

## Pair the two sides

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transfers in Settings">

Go to **Settings** > **Transfers**, or follow **Own-account transfers** at the bottom of **Payments**. Open **Pair a transfer** if it is closed.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the payment out">

In **Money left this account**, choose the outgoing payment. Each entry shows the date, account, payee and amount.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the payment in">

In **and arrived in this one**, choose the incoming payment in the other account. The dates do not have to be the same day.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note and confirm">

Fill in **Note**, for example "Savings top-up". It is required and is shown with the pair. Select **Confirm transfer pair**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

If the app refuses with "Transfer changed or unavailable; review both payments", one of the conditions above is not met: check the amounts, currencies, accounts, and that neither side is matched or already paired.

## Check that it worked

- On **Payments**, both sides show the stamp **Transfer** and a line such as "Own-account transfer · Main account → Savings · Savings top-up".
- The outgoing side no longer counts as missing a receipt.
- **Settings** > **Transfers** lists the pair.

Pairing never deletes or merges either payment. Both stay visible in their own accounts.

## Undo a pair

Select **Undo transfer** next to the pair on **Payments** or in **Settings** > **Transfers**. Both sides need a receipt again, and automatic matching runs. While a payment is paired, its other controls (such as **Not a purchase**) are not available.

## When pairing does not fit

A transfer in two currencies, a transfer with a fee taken off one side, or only one side imported cannot be paired. Mark the outgoing side **Not a purchase (no receipt needed)** on **Payments** instead. See [Match payments and receipts](/how-to/match-payments-and-receipts/).
