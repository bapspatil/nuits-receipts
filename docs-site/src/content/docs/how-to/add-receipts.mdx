Use this guide to get receipts into the app, one at a time or ten at once. Each file is read on its own, and anything that needs a fact from you waits in **Receipts To do**.

## Prerequisites

- A deployed copy you can sign in to. See [Deploy your own copy](/tutorials/deploy-your-own-copy/).
- Receipts as text PDFs (up to 10 MiB and 25 pages) or photos in PNG, JPEG or WebP (up to 4 MiB each).
- For email only: a receipt address with Email Routing and your sending address on the allowlist. See [Set up email](/how-to/set-up-email/).

![The Add page with the choice between receipts and invoices, a drop area for files and two files marked Added](../../../assets/screenshots/upload.png)

## Upload receipts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add">

Press **Add** on any page. On a computer it is the first button in the sidebar. On a phone it is the round button in the middle of the tab bar. On a brand new copy, **Home** also shows the same form under its setup checklist.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what the files are">

Under **What are these?**, keep **Receipts and bills** selected. Choose **Invoices you sent** only for sales invoices; see [Keep sales invoices](/how-to/keep-sales-invoices/). Choose **Other documents** for a contract or a letter that is kept but never read for amounts; see [Find any document](/how-to/find-any-document/).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the files">

Drop up to 10 files on the page or choose them, then select **Upload**. The list under the drop area shows each file as it is added, with a link to open it. A file that is too big or of the wrong type is reported on its own and the rest still go in. One file on its own opens its page once it is added.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Forward an email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Forward from an allowed address">

Forward the receipt email, or have your supplier send it, to your receipt address. The Add page shows that address when it is set.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Know what becomes a receipt">

Each PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP attachment (up to 10 per email) becomes its own receipt. An email with no supported attachment is kept as a receipt itself, saved from its HTML or text body. The email is the context the reader sees, so a short forwarding note such as the supplier's name can help.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Mail from an address that is not on your allowed senders waits at the top of **Receipts To do** as a **New sender** row, unread, until you allow or ignore the sender. Mail that fails the sender check is dropped. Mail that the app cannot prove came from you is kept, unread, with the flag **Is this email genuine?** The reasons are in [Email and sender trust](/explanation/email-and-sender-trust/).

## Check that it worked

- After a single upload you land on the receipt page. A heading such as **1 to check** means it waits in **Receipts To do**; with no heading and a **Save** button, it was read with no doubts.
- **Receipts** in the sidebar (or the tab bar on a phone) shows a count of what waits in To do.
- On **Home**, the **Check** row counts the receipts that need you, for example "1 to check, 1 undated". A receipt with no date counts as undated until you add one.

Uploading the exact same file again does not create a second receipt: it opens the one you already have. To handle near-duplicates, see [Resolve duplicate warnings](/how-to/resolve-duplicate-warnings/).
