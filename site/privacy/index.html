What this site collects

Nothing, with one exception. If you use the Motion switch in the menu, your choice ("on" or "off") is saved in your browser's localStorage as "motion", so the site remembers it. It isn't an identifier, it's never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Hosting

The site is static files served by Cloudflare Workers. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request data, such as your IP address, your browser's user agent and the page you asked for, to serve the site and protect it from abuse. Cloudflare does this as the host's processor. The legal basis is Nuits's legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Cloudflare's network is global, so this data may be processed outside the EU, under the transfer safeguards GDPR requires. Nuits keeps no logs of visits to this site.

Links to other sites

The Deploy button, the GitHub link and the licence link take you to other websites. Their own privacy policies apply there. Nothing on this site loads from them until you click.

The tool itself

If you deploy Nuits Receipts, it runs in your own Cloudflare account and sends nothing to Nuits. You are the controller for the receipts and data you put in it.
