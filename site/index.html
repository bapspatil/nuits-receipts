02Month end

Check, Match, Send. Then it's closed.

Home keeps the month as a strip of three steps above the archive. Each one shows what's left, and the strip empties as you go.

  1. Check

    Receipts To do lists only what needs you: a missing date, a VAT line that doesn't add up, mail from a new sender. Approve, and the next one moves up.

  2. Match

    Choose a bank statement (CSV, XLSX or PDF) and press Import statement. It finds the account and the columns, skips rows you imported before, and asks only when it can't tell. The whole import has an Undo.

  3. Send

    Download the month as a ZIP (every original, named by date and seller, plus an index spreadsheet) or just the CSV, then press I've sent it. A copy of what your accountant got stays on Home.

06Cost

AI reading: about 15 cents a month. Here's the arithmetic.

Clairic costs $0.10 per million input tokens. Clef costs $0.24 per million and only runs when no vendor rule matches. D1, R2 and Email Routing fit in Cloudflare's free allowances at this size. You pay your own Cloudflare bill.

One honest caveat. A PDF upload measured about 186 ms of CPU in production, far over the 10 ms the Workers Free plan allows. If you upload PDFs, plan on Workers Paid, a $5 a month minimum for the whole account. Forwarded email bodies may fit in Free.

07Limits

What it doesn't do.

I built this for my own receipts. Here's what it won't do for yours, at least not yet.

Nuits Receipts organises receipts and prepares a CSV for your accountant. It is not tax, VAT or accounting advice, and it does not do your bookkeeping. Figures are AI-extracted and you should check them.

  • It won't file anything for you. Not your taxes, not your receipts in a drawer.
  • It imports statements from banks that provide a CSV, XLSX or PDF export. Holvi exports are the most tested; other banks' files less so.
  • Search reads the words inside PDFs, not inside photos. A photo is found by the details it read.
  • It's for one person with one inbox.
  • It doesn't work offline. Installed, it shows a "You're offline" page and keeps nothing on the device.

08FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Which banks does it work with?

Banks that let you export a statement as CSV, XLSX or PDF. Choose the file and press Import statement: it finds the account and the columns, and asks only when it can't tell. Tested most with Holvi exports.

Can I find an old receipt?

Yes. Home keeps every finance document: receipts, sales invoices, bank statements and the month packages you sent. Search by seller, amount, date or any word inside a PDF, or open a folder for a year, a seller or a tag. Cmd+K (Ctrl+K off a Mac) finds a file from any page, and the PDF opens in the app.

What if I approve the wrong thing?

Press Undo in the banner, or u on a computer. Approve, Discard, Match, Not a purchase, Mark paid, a bank import and every bulk action can be undone. The few that can't, such as ignoring a sender, ask first.

Is there a phone app?

The web app installs on its own: Add to Home Screen on an iPhone or iPad, Add to Dock on a Mac, or Install app in Chrome and Edge. There's no app store. It needs a connection to work.

Does Nuits see my data?

No. It runs in your own Cloudflare account, with files in your R2 and data in your D1. Nuits never sees your data. Two calls go outside the Worker: Cloudflare Workers AI, where Clairic reads each receipt, and frankfurter.dev for exchange rates, which gets only a currency code and dates.

What do I need from Cloudflare?

An account, and a domain on Cloudflare if you want Email Routing to give you a receipt address. Uploads work without email. PDF uploads measured about 186 ms of CPU in production against the Workers Free plan's 10 ms limit, so plan on Workers Paid ($5 a month) if you upload PDFs.

Is this tax advice?

No. It organises receipts and prepares a CSV for your accountant. Figures are AI-extracted, so check them.

Is it a SaaS?

No. You deploy your own copy and nobody else runs it for you. It's open source under the Apache License 2.0.
