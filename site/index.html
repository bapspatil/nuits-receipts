01The problem

Receipts live everywhere except where you need them.

A PDF in your inbox. A photo on your phone. A paper one in a coat pocket. Then the month ends, the bank export has a line for every payment, and your accountant would like a receipt for each of them.

Nuits Receipts puts them in one inbox and tells you which ones you still owe.

02How it works

How it works

  1. 1 Step 1 of 3

    Send it in

    Forward a receipt to your inbox address, or upload a PDF or a photo.

  2. 2 Step 2 of 3

    It gets read

    Cloudflare's Clairic model on Workers AI (a preview model; its behaviour and pricing may change) reads the vendor, date, totals and VAT. Anything it isn't sure of goes to a review pile for you to check. When no vendor rule of yours matches, Cloudflare's Clef model picks which of your products and which expense category the receipt belongs to; rules you set always win.

  3. 3 Step 3 of 3

    It gets matched

    Import a CSV export from your bank; you map the columns once. It suggests matches between payments and receipts, flags payments with no receipt yet, and gives you a monthly cost view plus a CSV for your accountant.

05Cost

AI reading: about 15 cents a month. Here's the arithmetic.

Clairic costs $0.10 per million input tokens. Clef costs $0.24 per million and only runs when no vendor rule matches. D1, R2 and Email Routing fit in Cloudflare's free allowances at this size. You pay your own Cloudflare bill.

One honest caveat. A PDF upload measured about 186 ms of CPU in production, far over the 10 ms the Workers Free plan allows. If you upload PDFs, plan on Workers Paid, a $5 a month minimum for the whole account. Forwarded email bodies may fit in Free.

07Limits

What it doesn't do.

I built this for my own receipts. Here's what it won't do for yours, at least not yet.

Nuits Receipts organises receipts and prepares a CSV for your accountant. It is not tax, VAT or accounting advice, and it does not do your bookkeeping. Figures are AI-extracted and you should check them.

  • It won't file anything for you. Not your taxes, not your receipts in a drawer.
  • Bank CSV column mapping is new. It has been tested with Holvi exports so far, and other banks' files are less tested.
  • It's for one person with one inbox.
  • There's no mobile app.

08FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Which banks does it work with?

It imports CSV exports. There are presets for some banks and a column mapper for other layouts, so you map the columns once. Tested with Holvi exports so far.

Does Nuits see my data?

No. It runs in your own Cloudflare account, with files in your R2 and data in your D1. Nuits never sees your data. Two calls go outside the Worker: Cloudflare Workers AI, where Clairic reads each receipt, and frankfurter.dev for exchange rates, which gets only a currency code and dates.

What do I need from Cloudflare?

An account, and a domain on Cloudflare so Email Routing can give you an inbox address. PDF uploads measured about 186 ms of CPU in production against the Workers Free plan's 10 ms limit, so plan on Workers Paid ($5 a month) if you upload PDFs. Forwarded email bodies may fit in Free.

Is this tax advice?

No. It organises receipts and prepares a CSV for your accountant. Figures are AI-extracted, so check them.

Is it a SaaS?

No. You deploy your own copy and nobody else runs it for you.
